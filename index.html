Smooth Interpolation & Continuous Grading Curve

To prevent unrealistic rank gaps between adjacent marks (like jumping 8,000 ranks for a 1-mark difference), the calculation engine now uses a piecewise log-linear continuous interpolation across anchor points.

How scoring works: You enter your correct and wrong counts. Your net score out of 720 is calculated ($4 \times \text{Correct} - 1 \times \text{Wrong}$, with remaining questions treated as unattempted). The engine then smoothly interpolates your rank between adjacent reference brackets so every single mark change yields a realistic, proportionate rank shift.
