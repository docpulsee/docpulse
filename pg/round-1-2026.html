How to fill your choices well

  1. Fill more, not fewer. There is no limit that helps you by stopping early. Every realistic option you leave out is a seat you can't get.
  2. Order by preference, not by chance. You only get the highest choice you qualify for, so put the seat you want most first.
  3. Include DNB and deemed options you would genuinely join. In 2026 there are 9,201 DNB seats.
  4. Check fees, stipend and bond for every seat before you lock it.
  5. Lock before 10 AM on 22 Oct. Unlocked choices are auto-locked at the deadline.

Frequently asked questions

When is NEET PG 2026 Round 1 choice locking?

From 4 PM on 21 October to 10 AM on 22 October 2026, as per the MCC schedule.

When is the NEET PG 2026 Round 1 result?

The Round 1 result is scheduled for 24 October 2026, with reporting from 26 October to 2 November.

How many choices should I fill in NEET PG counselling?

Fill every seat you would genuinely join, in order of preference. Filling more realistic options never hurts your chances of a higher choice.

When does Round 2 start?

Round 2 registration runs from 6 to 11 November 2026, with choice locking from 4 PM on 11 November to 10 AM on 12 November.
