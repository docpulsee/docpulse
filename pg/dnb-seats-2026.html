The 2026 MCC seat matrix lists 9,201 DNB seats (DNB and DNB Diploma, NBEMS). Comparing it with 2025, DocPulsee finds about 550 DNB programmes that did not appear last year, and roughly 1,600 seats that are new or larger than before. More seats usually means DNB closing ranks can go later than last year.

Frequently asked questions

How many DNB seats are there in NEET PG 2026?

The MCC 2026 seat matrix lists 9,201 DNB seats (DNB and DNB Diploma), of which about 2,582 are in government hospitals.

Are there new DNB seats in 2026?

Yes. Compared with 2025, DocPulsee counts about 550 DNB programmes that are new this year and roughly 1,600 seats that are new or bigger, led by Anaesthesiology, OBG, Paediatrics and Ophthalmology.

What rank is needed for DNB in NEET PG 2026?

Based on MCC 2025 closing ranks adjusted for 2026 seats, a General category rank of 50,000 could reach about 340 DNB options, 75,000 about 61 and 1 lakh about 19. Reserved categories reach more.

Do DNB seats get added in later rounds?

Often, yes. In MCC 2025, 479 DNB seats were added in Round 2.

When is DNB choice locking for Round 1 in 2026?

DNB seats are filled through MCC counselling. Round 1 choice locking is from 4 PM on 21 October to 10 AM on 22 October 2026.
