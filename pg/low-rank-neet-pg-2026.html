Frequently asked questions

Can I get a PG seat with a NEET PG rank of 1 lakh?

Based on MCC 2025 closing ranks adjusted for 2026 seats, a General category rank of 1 lakh could reach about 811 college-branch options, including about 311 in the All India quota and 19 DNB options. Reserved categories reach more.

What can I get with a rank of 1.5 lakh in NEET PG?

About 294 options in General category last year, including about 80 in the All India quota, 7 DNB and 207 deemed university options.

Should I drop a year if my NEET PG rank is low?

Look at the data first. Around 3,050 seats were added after Round 1 in MCC 2025, and later rounds often open new options. Check how many seats your own rank and category can reach before deciding.

Do seats increase after Round 1?

Yes. In MCC 2025, about 2,750 seats were added in Round 2 and about 300 more in Round 3.
