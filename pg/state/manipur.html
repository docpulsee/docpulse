Key rules from the official Manipur brochure

MeritSelection is based on NEET-PG-2025 merit.
Seat distributionJNIMS 2025: 91 PG seats, AIQ 44 and State Quota 47 (46+1).
State categoriesState quota is filled through In-Service, Direct and Institutional Preference categories under the Manipur Medical Postgraduate Seat Allotment Schemes, 2025.
In-serviceIn-service sponsored category is open to NEET-PG qualified medical officers from RIMS beneficiary states, with applications forwarded by competent authority.
Non-RIMS OpenNEET-PG qualified candidates who completed MBBS elsewhere and belong to RIMS beneficiary states are eligible for Non-RIMS Open category.
