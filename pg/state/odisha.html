Key rules from the official Odisha brochure

SC/ST migrantsSC/ST persons who migrated from their State of Origin to another State for education or employment are deemed SC/ST of the State of origin and get the privilege from that state, not the state they migrated to.
SC/ST certificateSC and ST reserved seats are filled by applicants of the respective community per the Odisha Caste Certificate (for S.C. and S.T.) Rules-1980 as amended, and are not interchangeable.
PwD eligibilityPwD reserved seats are filled by persons with benchmark disabilities under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016, on the NEET PG merit list, with the disability certificate obtained only from MCC/GOI notified centres listed in the guidelines.
Green Card claimGreen Card candidates must produce the parents' original Green Card, which must not have been used before for reservation, and its facts must tally with other certificates; no affidavit is entertained for mismatches, and the candidate is then treated as unreserved.
Ex-servicemen eligibilityEx-servicemen category candidates must be a Permanent Resident of Odisha and the son, daughter or spouse of a defence service person, or have served in defence service themselves, with those who served themselves considered first.
