Key rules from the official Meghalaya brochure

InternshipCompulsory one-year internship must be completed on or before 15-Aug-2025 for NEIGRIHMS PG admission.
NEIGRIHMS Northeast eligibilityNEIGRIHMS 2025 PG prospectus recognizes permanent residents/domiciles of the eight North-Eastern states including Sikkim for Northeast open-category seats; it is an institutional/regional PG route, not evidence of a separate state PG counselling quota.
NEIGRIHMS seat structureNEIGRIHMS 2025 prospectus states 50% seats are All India Quota and the remaining 50% are Northeast open category, with 25% of overall seats reserved for NEIGRIHMS MBBS graduates.
Separate state PG brochureNo separate 2025 state PG medical counselling brochure/rule set was identified in the official source set reviewed; therefore no state-specific quota/fee/bond figures have been invented. NEIGRIHMS/RIMS regional routes are recorded separately where applicable.
