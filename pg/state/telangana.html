Key rules from the official Telangana brochure

Cutoff 2025General/EWS 50th percentile, score 276; General PwD 45th percentile, score 255; SC/ST/OBC including PwD 40th percentile, score 235 in the 2025 registration notice
Management categoriesMQ-1 and MQ-2 (NRI) categories are explicitly referenced for management quota web options
TuitionManagement quota tuition fee is paid at respective colleges and is as notified by Telangana Government orders
Web optionsEligible candidates may exercise web options for any number of subjects/colleges according to eligibility and registered categories
Registration and processing feeFor PG Medical Degree/Diploma Competent Authority Quota 2025-26, registration/processing fee is Rs 10,000 for OC and BC candidates and Rs 8,000 for SC/ST candidates, plus bank transaction charges.
