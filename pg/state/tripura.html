Key rules from the official Tripura brochure

BasicRecognized MBBS, NEET-PG qualification and one-year compulsory internship completed by 31-Jul-2025; medical registration required.
In-serviceTripura domicile, MBBS from Tripura or outside, NOC from appointing authority and service certificate for applicable incentive weightage.
Non-government TMCTripura permanent resident OR ex-student who passed MBBS from AGMC or Tripura Medical College, irrespective of domicile.
State quotaCitizen of India, Tripura domicile, and MBBS from an NMC-recognized medical college.
NEET PGGeneral/EWS/WESM 50th percentile; SC/ST including PwD/WESM of SC/ST 40th; UR PwD 45th.
