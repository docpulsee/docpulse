Key rules from the official Sikkim brochure

SMIMS MD/MS application 2026-27Sikkim Manipal Institute of Medical Sciences (SMU): last date to apply or correct the MD/MS online application for Round 1 is 25 Oct 2026 (midnight). Counselling will be online; dates to be announced on the SMU notice board.
SMIMS fraud cautionSMIMS deals with no agents or consultants; admission is only through the candidate's SMIMS portal login. Official contacts: admission.smims@smu.edu.in, dean@smims.smu.edu.in, 9083618855 / 03592-270389.
SMIMS mandatory uploadsNEET-PG score card, admit card and other required documents must be uploaded, and NEET score and rank filled in the form, by 25 Oct 2026 midnight; otherwise the application is left out of the merit list.
InternshipCompulsory one-year internship must be completed on or before 15-Aug-2025 for NEIGRIHMS PG admission.
NEIGRIHMS Northeast eligibilityNEIGRIHMS 2025 PG prospectus recognizes permanent residents/domiciles of the eight North-Eastern states including Sikkim for Northeast open-category seats; it is an institutional/regional PG route, not evidence of a separate state PG counselling quota.
