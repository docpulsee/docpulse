Key rules from the official Tamil Nadu brochure

Application dates – allOnline application opens 09.10.2026 at 12:00 Noon and closes 15.10.2026 at 5:00 PM (server time) for GQ, MQ and DNB; there is no extension and no submission after closing.
NEET PG requirementCandidates must qualify in NEET PG 2026 conducted by the National Board of Examinations; DGHS/NMC eligibility criteria apply.
NationalityCandidates should be citizens of India; OCI cardholders are treated on par with Indian citizens and are eligible for Open (General) category seats, as per the Supreme Court order dated 03.02.2023 in WP No. 891 of 2021.
TN native – GQTamil Nadu natives who did MBBS including internship (CRMI/CRRI) either in Tamil Nadu or outside the state can apply under their community for Government college seats and Government quota seats in self-financing colleges.
Other-state native – GQNatives of other states who did MBBS including internship in Tamil Nadu are eligible only under the open category of Government and Government quota seats.
