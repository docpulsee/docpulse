Key rules from the official Madhya Pradesh brochure

MD/MS eligibility (2025 amended)Candidate must have the minimum NEET-PG percentile set by NMC for their category, an MBBS from an NMC-recognised college (or an NBE screening test pass if the qualification is from outside India), a completed internship per NMC norms, permanent/temporary MP Medical Council registration, and an MBBS degree from an NMC-recognised medical college in Madhya Pradesh (amendment of 03-09-2025, replacing the earlier entry).
MDS eligibility (2025 amended)Candidate must have the minimum NEET-MDS percentile set by DCI for their category, a BDS from a DCI-recognised dental college (or an NBE screening test pass if qualified outside India), DCI-norm internship, MP Dental Council registration, and a BDS degree from a DCI-recognised dental college in Madhya Pradesh (amendment of 03-09-2025).
MP MBBS/BDS condition relaxedFrom round 2 and later rounds, if no registered candidates with an MP MBBS (MD/MS) or MP BDS (MDS) degree are available, this condition is automatically relaxed for the vacancies then available (as printed in the 03-09-2025 amendment).
Earlier domicile requirement (2018)The original 2018 Schedule-1 entry for Diploma/MD/MS and MDS also required the candidate to be a local resident (मूल निवासी) of Madhya Pradesh, relaxed from round 2 onwards if no MP residents were available; the 03-09-2025 replacement entries for MD/MS and MDS list MBBS/BDS from MP instead.
Repeat PG admission gapA candidate is automatically ineligible to take admission in a different subject's Diploma/PG course for 2 years from the date of passing a Diploma exam, and for 3 years from the date of passing a postgraduate exam (Schedule-1, Sl. 5).
