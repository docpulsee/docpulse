Key rules from the official West Bengal brochure

State quota counsellingState Quota PG medical seats for MD/MS/Diploma and DNB are allotted through online state counselling.
DownloadsOfficial portal publishes Information Bulletin, participating institutes, round-wise seat matrices, candidate registration manual and choice-filling manual
Seat matricesRound-1, Round-2, Round-3 and Stray Vacancy seat matrices plus registration and choice-filling manuals are published.
Degree in-service quota40% of State Quota degree seats are reserved for in-service teachers and medical officers of WBMES/WBHS/WBPHAS.
Diploma in-service quota50% of State Quota diploma seats are reserved for in-service teachers and medical officers of WBMES/WBHS/WBPHAS.
