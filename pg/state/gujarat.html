Key rules from the official Gujarat brochure

Eligibility 2026-27MD/MS/Diploma via ACPPGMEC: MBBS from a university established under Gujarat law and located in Gujarat; OR MBBS from any university in India/abroad meeting NMC criteria plus Class 12 (B or AB group) from Gujarat Board, or CBSE/CISCE/IB/Cambridge/NIOS from a school located in Gujarat, and born in Gujarat or Gujarat domicile.
Internship cut-offCompulsory rotating internship must be completed on or before 30-09-2026.
Documents (ACPPGMEC)All attempt-wise MBBS marksheets; NEET-PG marksheet; internship completion certificate (by 30-09-2026); provisional or permanent medical council registration; birth date/place and Indian citizenship proof (school leaving/transfer certificate, plus birth certificate or passport if birthplace is missing); passport copy if dual/foreign citizenship.
Category certificatesSC/ST/SEBC caste certificate and SEBC non-creamy layer (Parishishta 4) and EWS certificates must be from Gujarat competent authorities: Gujarati certificates issued on/after 01-04-2024, English certificates on/after 01-04-2026. PwD: disability certificate from competent authority.
MBBS outside GujaratCandidates with MBBS from outside Gujarat also submit the 12th marksheet, and a Gujarat domicile certificate if their birthplace is outside Gujarat.
