Key rules from the official Kerala brochure

Academic qualificationApplicants need an MBBS degree or provisional pass certificate recognised by NMC (MCI), registered permanently or provisionally with NMC (MCI) or a State Medical Council, with one year of internship completed or likely to be completed on or before 30/09/2026. Admission is given only after obtaining registration with Kerala State Medical Council/erstwhile T.C. Medical Council.
Nativity - Kerala originIndian citizens of Kerala origin are eligible. Only Indian citizens are eligible for professional courses unless otherwise notified.
OCI/PIO candidatesOCI cardholders are treated at par with Indian citizens and are eligible for UR (General) seats and NRI seats in counselling. PIO/OCI candidates are not eligible for any kind of reservation.
Non-Keralite candidatesSons/daughters of Non-Keralite parents who obtained MBBS from any Medical College in Kerala are eligible, but get no Communal/Special/PD reservation benefits. They must execute a bond to serve as Senior Resident for one year.
Non-Keralite in-service doctorsFollowing Kerala High Court judgment in WP(C) 24607/2023 dated 07.03.24, the nativity clause is void for service quota, so Non-Keralite in-service doctors are eligible to apply under service quota.
