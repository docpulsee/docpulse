Key rules from the official Maharashtra brochure

Applicant poolNEET-PG qualified candidates including NRI and In-service candidates may register subject to required credentials
EWS certificateFor Maharashtra admission, EWS certificate must be in the State format specified in the information brochure; Central-format EWS certificate is not accepted.
Category change after submissionA candidate must claim the reservation category in the application before submission; conversion from Open/General to a reserved category after submission is not entertained.
NRI quotaNRI candidates must register on the Foreign Candidate Registration Portal before the notified deadline and are eligible for 15% Institute Quota seats as per merit.
PwD certificatePwD candidates should have claimed PwD quota at NEET registration and must submit an online PwD certificate issued by one of the 16 designated boards.

All rules, documents, bond and penalties are in the DocPulsee Rule Book.
