Key rules from the official Haryana brochure

RegistrationCandidate should be registered with a State Medical Council/NMC and satisfy NEET-PG minimum eligibility
Government institutions50% seats All-India Merit Category and 50% Haryana State Merit Category
Private institutions50% Open Merit/State Quota and 50% Management category
In-serviceUp to 40% of State Quota seats in all PG specialties reserved as in-service seats for eligible Haryana Government/ESIC/other specified state-service candidates with valid NOC
Institutional preference25% State Quota seats reserved for candidates who passed MBBS from institutions located in Haryana
