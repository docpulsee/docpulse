Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

DocPulsee ("we", "our", "us") provides a NEET PG counselling directory and calculator at docpulsee.in. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account info: when you sign in (via email or Google), we receive your email address and, if using Google Sign-In, your basic Google profile info (name, email). We use this only to identify your account and check whether you've unlocked paid access.
  • Payment info: payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not store your card, UPI, or bank details — only a payment confirmation ID.
  • Usage data: anonymous ratings you submit about institutes (work culture, hours, hands-on exposure) are stored to show aggregate crowd feedback to other users.
  • Choice List Maker: when you make a choice list while signed in, we store your rank, category and your top choices (one list per account, replaced when you change it). We use them only to show anonymous demand trends ("heating up" / "cooling") to other users. Individual lists, and how many lists we hold, are never shown to anyone.
  • Accuracy record (optional): if you tick "Add my seat to DocPulsee's public accuracy record" in My Counselling Plan, we store the seat and round you were allotted. We compare it with the chances we showed you and publish only combined results (for example, "when we said 70%, it happened 72%") once enough students have taken part. You can untick it at any time to remove your seat.

How we use your information

  • To let you sign in and access paid content you've purchased.
  • To send you sign-in links/codes and, if you book one, details about a 1:1 counselling session.
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Senior Connect

Senior Connect lists last year's candidates only by the seat they were allotted in MCC counselling (college, branch, quota and round). We never show a senior's name or phone number publicly. We share them with a single student only after the senior has personally agreed. Any senior who wants their listing removed can email docpulsee@gmail.com and we'll remove it.

Third-party services we use

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting.
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in method.
  • Razorpay — payment processing.
  • SendGrid — sending sign-in emails.

Data retention

We retain your account and payment-verification data for as long as your account exists. You can request deletion of your data by emailing us at the address below.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email docpulsee@gmail.com.
