Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Full directory access (₹3,900, or the pre-counselling price of ₹1,999 until 15 Oct 2026)

  • Try before you pay. You can see real results for your rank and a full sample college for free, so you know exactly what you're buying before you pay.
  • Once College Directory access is unlocked, the payment is non-refundable. It's a digital product: all data is visible the moment you pay, so we can't take it back.
  • We refund in full if you were charged twice for the same thing, or if a technical problem on our side stops your access from working and we can't fix it within 48 hours. Email docpulsee@gmail.com from the email you paid with.

1:1 counselling session (₹2,000, or ₹799 for College Directory members)

  • Full refund if we cancel your session or can't hold it at a time that suits you.
  • Full refund if you cancel at least 12 hours before the call time we agreed with you.

Senior Connect (₹999 per senior)

  • You pay only after the senior has agreed to talk to you.
  • Full refund if the number doesn't work, or the senior doesn't reply within 48 hours of your payment — or, if you prefer, we'll connect you with another senior instead.
  • Once you've spoken with the senior, the payment is non-refundable.

DocPulse Pro

  • Pro includes full directory access, two 1:1 counselling calls, two Senior Connects, one Route Finder report and the Doctor DNA test. The calls, Senior Connects and report can be used until 31 March 2027.
  • The College Directory part of Pro follows the College Directory rules above.
  • We refund in full if you were charged twice for Pro, or if a technical problem on our side stops Pro from working and we can't fix it within 48 hours.
  • If we can't hold a call at a time that suits you, or a senior doesn't reply within 48 hours, that call or Senior Connect is given back to you to use again.

Other purchases

Smaller purchases (such as the ₹149 branch test) are non-refundable once the result is shown. If you paid and the test didn't open, or you were charged twice, email us within 7 days and we'll refund you in full.

Referral rewards

Rewards are paid within 7 days of your friend's payment. If that payment is refunded (for example, a duplicate charge), the ₹300 reward for it is cancelled.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay. Once we approve a refund, it usually reaches you within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank. Please include your Razorpay Payment ID (starts with "pay_") in your email if you have it.
