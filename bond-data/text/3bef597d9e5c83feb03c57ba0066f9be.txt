Bond and Penalty
A} After the successful completion of the post graduate course; the
candidates, if required by the institute, shall be issued appointment
order by authorities of the Institute. The candidate is required to join
| within 5 days after issue of order for a minimum uninterrupted
period of 365 days. In case of failing to join the post within 5 days,
and / or not serving the Institute for 365 working days, the candidate
will be required to pay Rs. 50 lacs to the Institute i.e. Mahatma Gandhi
Institute of Medical sciences, Sevagram. Duration of service period
will be extended without pay proportionately in order to complete the
365 working days after completion of initial 380 days from the date of
issue of appointment order and he/she will be required to sign a bond
of Rs.80 lacs along with cheque of the same amount to be deposited
in case of default to this effect at the time of joining the postgraduate
course.
B} Candidate who has joined for postgraduate course should complete
Junior Residency tenure, failing which he/ she will be required to pay
Rs. 0,00,000/- ( Rs. Ten Lakhs only) (i.e. for non completion of Junior
Residency tenure) , .

have to pay an additional penalty of Rs.0,00,000/- (Rs. Ten Lakhs only)
