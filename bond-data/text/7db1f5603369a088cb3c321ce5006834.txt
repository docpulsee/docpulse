SUBJECT: Admission to Post MBBS DNB (Degree) and Post MBBS Diploma
courses in District Hospitals in the State of Punjab - Session 2023
onwards.

. The Government of Punjab is pleased to notify policy for admissions to Post
MBBS DNB and Post MBBS Diploma courses in the District Hospitals in the State
of Punjab for the year 2023 onwards.

2. All admissions of Govt. and Private institutions, including private/deemed
universities and minority institutions shall be based on merit of National
Eligibility cum EntranceTest (Post Graduate) (NEET-PG)

3. The eligibility criteria and other conditions shall be as prescribed in information
Bulletin for National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (Post Graduate) for admission to
Post MBBS DNB/Diploma courses issued by National Board of examination, New
Delhi.

4. The Government of Punjab is further pleased to authorize Baba Farid University of
Health Sciences (BFUHS), Faridkot to conduct centralized counselling for
admission to 50% State (in service) Quota seats in District Hospitals of the State
of Punjab, on the basis of Merit of NEET-PG as per procedure laid down by
National Medical Commission of india. The process and schedule of counselling
shall be decided by Baba Farid University of Health Sciences, Faridkot The
details shall be published by the Baba Farid University of Health Sciences,
Faridkot. (www. bfuhs.ac.in)

6. One year compulsory rotatory intemship is an essential prequalification for
acquiring eligibility for admission to Post Graduate courses and the candidate
‘should be registered with the State Medical Council. The cutoff date for completion
of Internship shall be as decided/ixed by NMC. However, the condition of one
year compulsory rotatory internship is not applicable to those candidates who
have been specifically exempted

6. A candidate who, after passing the final qualifying examination (MBBS or the
foreign Medical Degree recognized by GOUNMC), Is undergoing 2-month
compulsory rotatory Internship/practical training and is likely to complete the same
by date as decided by NMC. shall be eligible for admission provided he/she
completes the compulsory rotatory internship and obtains provisional or
permanent registration. Such a candidate shall produce a certificate from the Head
of Institution that he/she will be completing internship by the date decided by NMC.

7, The tentative distribution of seats provided by State institute of Health andFamily
Welfare, Punjab shail be displayed by Baba Farid University of Health Sciences,
