AND WHEREAS, in the Interest of public service, the First Party has decided to give

admission to the Second Party in the Post Graduate course/Post Graduate Diploma Course

in the above noted State Medical College Hospital of Assam, namely Assam Medical

College at Dibrugarh in the State of Assam, and the First Party shall bear the expenses

of his/her education, other than the admission fees, hostel fees and other charges, in

pursuing said Post Graduate course/Post Graduate Diploma Course;

NOW THEREFORE, THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH THE FOLLOWIWG TERMS

AND CONDITIONS:

i. That after completion of his/her Post Graduate Degree Course / Post Graduate Diploma
Course, the Second Party shall have to compulsorily serve the Government of Assam for a
mandatory period of 2 (two) years, following the successful completion of his/her
degree/diploma course.

2. That the First Party shall have the authority to utilize, the service of the student i.e. the
Second Party in any Hospital/Medical institution within the State of Assam where there is
necessity of a Specialist Doctor in the interest of public service, including any eligible
agency notified by the Health and Family Welfare Department, and/or any other
State/Central Government sponsored scheme/s, or, in any part of the State of Assam, if
the Government desires his/her service.

3. That Second Party shall be governed by the Medical Colleges of Assam (Regulation of
Admission into Post Graduate Degree and Diploma Courses) Rules, 202i, as amended up
to date.

4... That in case of any breach of the terms and conditions as stated hereinabove, the Second
Party shall be bound to pay an amount of Rs. 20,00,000/- (Rupees Twenty Lakhs) only, in
respect of Post Graduate Degree Candidate, or, Rs. 5,00,000/- (Rupees Fifteen Lakhs)
only in respect of Post Graduate Diploma Candidate as per Rule 9(i) of Medical Colleges
of Assam (Regulation of Admission into Post Graduate Degree and Diploma
Courses) Rules, 2023, to the First Party, i.e., the Government of Assam, Health and
Family Welfare Department, as compensate on account of expenses borne for the Second
Party in pursuing the aforesaid Post Graduate Course.

5. That in case of failure to pay the compensation by the Second Party as mentioned above,
the First Party shall be at liberty to file Money Suit and/or to take any other appropriate
legal action against the Second Party in the competent court at Guwahati, Assam, for
recovering the same at the risk and cost of the Second Party.

IN WITNESSES WHEREOF, the parties hereto, having fully understood the terms and

conditions of this Agreement and with full knowledge and sound mind have signed, sealed and

delivered these presents on the day, month and year as mentioned above.
