LIQUIDATED DAMAGES & EXECUTION OF BOND FOR POSTGRADUATE
MEDICAL COURSES IN MALABAR CANCER CENTRE(PGIOSR)
‘The candidates selected for Medical PG Degree courses under National Medical Commission
( NMC) will have to execute two bonds in stamp paper (all pages of the bond should be in
stamp paper of the total value of Rs.200/-of Kerala stamp paper) each at the time of joining
the course to the effect that;
He/she shall not discontinue the P.G course that has been allotted to him/her after the
allotment Process is completed, If the candidate discontinues the course after completion of
allotment process, a liquidated damage of Rs.50 lakhs should be paid to the institution
All the General merit candidates including AIQ candidates admitted to Medical Postgraduate
Courses in Malabar Cancer Centre ( Postgraduate Institute of Oncology Sciences)
[MCC(PGIOSR)}, Thalassery, Kerala shall serve as Senior Resident as decided by the
Director, MCC(PGIOSR) for a continuous period of not less than One year immediately after
the completion of the course without break. Bonded Obligation can be fulfilled as service as
Senior Resident.
However the period of senior residency will be only from the date of publication of the result
subject to successful completion of course. Terms and conditions including remuneration will
be decided by the MCC (PGIOSR). In case the doctor gets selected for PG Degree courses in
Super Specialties in MCC(PGIOSR) he/she shall be relieved to join the same when he/she
will be expected to complete 2 years of bonded service after the completion of Super-
specialty course successfully. The total service rendered by himvher shall be including
obligations of specialty and super-specialty courses. The total bonded service rendered by
him/her shall not exceed 2 years. This facility of relieving shall be made available only for
Super Specialty degree courses and not for observer ship, fellowship or any other courses. In
case, he/she received admission to super-specialty course in a Government/Private institution,
he/she shall complete one year of bond in MCC (PGIOSR) if there is no mandatory bond in

‘Those who get a regular appointment in the services of the State of Kerala & other States,
Central Government, Union Territories and PSUs (under Kerala Government - _—
Government) shall be relieved from the Compulsory Bonded Service without insisting
bonded obligation, provided that they produce a letter from the concerned appointing
authority, stating that he or she is not entitled for extension of joining time along with the
appointment letter. He/she should also furnish a copy of the joining order served by the
appropriate authority within a month to the Director, MCC(PGIOSR), failing which they will
have to pay the liquidated damages as per the bond, The Senior Resident who is appointed to
the Government service must apply for a Joining Time Extension for completion of the
residency period and if the employer refuses to do so, the Senior Resident shall submit the
application before the Director, MCC(PGIOSR) to relieve him/her from Senior Residency.
Reliable documents to prove the above should be submitted along with the application.
All other senior residents shall be relieved only after | year and such service should be done
without any break immediately after the course. . In case the Senior Resident avails maternity
benefits during the period of Senior Residency. the period will be limited to [8 months which
is inclusive of the maximum maternity leave period of 6 months. No stipend will be paid
during the matemity leave

No admission shall be valid without getting the bond as detailed above and executed at the
time of joining or within 7days from the date of admission at the discretion of the Director,
MCC-PGIOSR. After 7 days the admission is liable to be cancelled. Candidates getting
admission in the final allotment will have to execute the bond within 7 days, Otherwise their
admission shall be cancelled and they are bound to pay the liquidated damages of Rs.
30,00,000/-.If candidates violate the terms above it will be construed as Professional
Misconduct and the fact reported to the Kerala State Medical Council / erstwhile T.C.
Medical Council and National Medical Commission for suitable action including cancellation
of Registration by the Council. In addition to the liquidated damages the stipend/salary drawn
during the period of the course and the amount spent by MCC(PGIOSR) for their studies
(with interest as fixed by MCC(PGIOSR) will be levied as liquidated damages from those
who fail to do the Government service as stipulated.
