Whereas the candidate has on his/her application, been selected for nomination against a seat

reserved for the All India Quota/State Quota, to undergo study for the Post Graduate MD/MS course

under the department of at the College.

And whereas the candidate has in consideration of his/her nomination agreed to complete this study

for the said course at the said College, subject to the conditions hereinafter appearing as below:-

NOW, THEREFORE, this indenture of the agreement witnessed and it is hereby agreed as follows:

i. The candidate shall abide by the rules of the said College for the time being in force regulating the
conduct of student at the said College and shall pass all the Examinations prescribed for the said
course by the University of the Medical Institute concerned.

2. If the candidate leaves the said College before passing the final examination without the prior
permission of the Government in writing for any reason whatsoever including illness or if he/she is
discharged or dismissed for misconduct or if he otherwise fails to complete the Bond year under
clause 3 hereof, he will be liable to pay to the College on demand and without a demur such sums
paid to him as stipend and book grant with an interest of 5% thereon as the Government may direct
within a period of (one) year. The decision of the College under this clause as to the commission of
a breach, as also the compensation payable in respect thereof shall be final and binding on the
candidate.

3. Upon passing the POST GRADUATE DOCTOR OF MEDICINE/MASTER OF SURGERY course, the
candidate shall serve as a Resident Doctor of the prescribed period as laid down by the appropriate
Authority as the case may be.


4. If any dispute arises between the parties hereto in respect of his/her agreement or any of the
provisions here in contained or anything arising here in, the decision of the College shall be final and
binding.

5. Every candidate shall serve a compulsory (one) year posting on completion of their respective
course(s) if College so desires. The tenure of rural posting of one year shall include serving under any
health care centre/institution run by the Government/Churches/Organisation/Societies as deemed
appropriate by the Government of Mizoram, provided that prior permission should be obtained
from the Competent Authority in the Health & Family Welfare Department.

In case of failure to comply with the agreement, the candidate will be liable to pay a sum of Rs.
20,00,000/- (Rupees Twenty Lakhs) to the College.
