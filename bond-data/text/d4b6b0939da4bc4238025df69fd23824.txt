FACULTY OF MEDICINE ALIGARH MUSLIM UNIVERSITY, ALIGARH
TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF ADMISSION AS JUNIOR RESIDENT

The admission to post graduate Degree/Diploma Courses will be governed by the
concerned Chapters of Ordinances (Academic) Chapter XLIII to L for all academic matters,
during the period of admission as Junior Residents. The terms and conditions of admission shall
be as follows:

l. TENURE

i) The tenure of those admitted to post graduate degree course will be 36 calendar months
from the date of admission and shall be 24 months for those admitted to diploma courses.

ii) The report of progress will be monitored by the Chairman of the department and will be
forwarded to Dean, Faculty of Medicine every three months.

iii) | Admission to Degree/Diploma Course will initially be provisional and may be cancelled
at any time owing to reasonable occasion:

(a) By the AMU, without any previous notice if the Chairman of the department/Dean,

faculty of Medicine are satisfied, on Medical evidence, that the student is unfit and is likely to

continue unfit for a considerable period of reason if ill health for the discharge of his/ her duties,

PROVIDED ALWAYS that the above decision that the post graduate student is unfit and
is likely to continue unfit, shall be final and binding on him/her.

(b) By the AMU, Aligarh or its authorized officer without any previous notice if the post
graduate student is found guilty of non-performance, derelication of duties, misbehavior,
indiscipline and non-punctuality or any other misconduct as laid down in the AMU Students
Conduct and Discipline Rules 985, in force at present or subsequently enforced.

2. STIPEND:

i) The amount of monthly stipend shall be fixed in accordance with the notification from
the UGC.

ii) An amount of 5. ]000/- will be deducted from the first payment as security deposit and
the same will be returned after completion of course on receipt of clearance certificate from the
Dean of the faculty of Medicine.

iii) If the post graduate student is suspended from the duty in connection with any
investigation into his/her conduct, he/she shall not be entitled to any stipend during such period
of suspension but shall receive a suitable amount as may be decided by the Dean, Faculty of
Medicine.

“The stipend to PG students may be awarded for 24 months in case of Diploma and 36
months in case of Degree within a maximum period of 05 years, from the date of joining, if they
avail any kind of leave except E/L & CL.”

3. WORKING HOURS:

Hours of work will be from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. for clinical departments. Besides
night calls, OPD and Emergency calls to Casualty Section will have to be attended as arranged
by the Chairman of department. The daily work and teaching programme will be arranged by
the Chairman of the department and any additional work due to exigencies in the department
may be assigned by the Chairman of the department. Hours of work for non-clinical department
shall be by the Chairman of the departments.

4. MEDICAL CARE:

Medical Care will be the same as are available to the other students of the University.
5. CERTIFICATION:

Certification of completion of course will be issued, only after receipt of a satisfactory
work and conduct report from the Chairman of the department concerned and after obtaining a
‘No Dues Certificate’ from the concerned University offices, Dean’s Office, faculty Library,
Dept./Hospital/Hall of residence etc.

6. OBSERVANCE OF RULES AND REGULATIONS:

i) Every post graduate student shall be required to abide by all rules and reregulations and
orders, prescribed for them from time to time by the University Faculty of Medicine, J.N.
Medical College Hospital.

ii) Every post graduate student shall devote his/her whole time in such duties as are
prescribed in pursuance of his/her course of study and shall not engage himself/herself, directly
or indirectly, in any private practice, trade business, occupation or other profession. Violation
of rules shall be treated as misconduct and shall make him/her liable to disciplinary action.
