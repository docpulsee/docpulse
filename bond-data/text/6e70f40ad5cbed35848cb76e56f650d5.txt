To, in favour of NAMO Medical Education & Research Institute, Silvassa whereas the
party of the FIRST PART has applied and admitted through the post graduate (PG)
counselling being conducted by Medical Counselling Committee (MCC), New Delhi.
Whereas on the basis of the PG merit, the student was offered various course/s at various
institution/s available at the time of his/her counselling conducted by Medical
Counselling Committee (MCC), New Delhi and he/she has voluntarily opted for the
course at NAMO Medical Education & Research Institute, Silvassa and he/she has been
admitted in the course at this institute with the understanding and subject to the
undertaking that the student shall undergo the course on full time and regular basis.
Whereas the party of the FIRST PART has agreed to serve the U.T. Administration of
Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu or any other place in the entire country as
specified by the UT Administration of Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu for a
period not less than three years after successful completion of the Post Graduate
(MD/MS) course and on such failure of not completing the full bond period of three
years, the party of the FIRST PART shall forthwith pay a sum of Rs.50,00,000/-
(Rs. Fifty Lakhs only).
During the above period, he/she will be paid a consolidated remuneration fixed by the
UT Administration/State Government/Central Government, as the case may be. In case
any person refuses or fails to serve for full or part of the period demanded or is removed
by the UT | Administration/State | Government/Central Government for
incompetence/indiscipline or misconduct, he/she shall pay to the U.T. Administration of
Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu on demand and without a demur a sum not
exceeding _Rs.50,00,000/-(Rupees Fifty Lakh only). The decision of the
U.T. Administration of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu as to the commission
of a breach as also to the amount of compensation payable in respect thereof shall be final
and binding on the applicant.
Any outstanding amount due from the applicant under this clause may be recovered from
him/her or legal heirs as arrears of land revenue.
AND WHEREAS for the better protection of the Government, the party of the FIRST
PART has agreed to execute the bond with 3 sureties who are Income Tax assesses to
stand guarantee of the above said amount of Rs.50,00,000/- (Rs. Fifty Lakhs only).
AND WHEREAS the party of the FIRST PART have also agreed that on successful
completion of the Post Graduate (MD/MS) course/s, his/her certificates relating to MBBS
& Post Graduate (MD/MS) course/s will not be given to the party of the FIRST PART
unless the party of the FIRST PART successfully completes the service bond for a period
of three years or pay to the UT Administration ( hereinafter called “The Government) on
demand the sum of Rs.50,00,000/- (Rs. Fifty Lakhs only) and on such default together
with interest at Government rates thereon from the date of demand on the said amount.

last round of counselling for admission. This penalty shall also, be applicable to the
