| MCG/Bond/Policy/AIQ/AI/2800-/2022, Office of the Commissioner of Health, Medical Services,
and Medical Education, Block No, 4, Third Floor, Dr. Jivraj Mehta Bhavan, Gandhinagar, dated
|4/09/2022, the bond policy applicable to students admitted under the State Quota will also apply to
students admitted under the All India Quota for undergraduate and postgraduate medical courses in the
state starting from the academic year 2022-23. Thus, students admitted under the All India Quota from
the academic year 2022-23 will also be required to execute a bond for serving a specified period.

l. Students admitted to the first year of postgraduate medical courses in government medical
colleges will be required to submit a bond of %40.00 lakh (Rupees Forty Lakh) to the Dean of the
respective medical college at the time of admission, as a guarantee to serve in rural areas under
the state government for a minimum period of one year after completing their course.

2. Postgraduate students will be required to provide either a bank guarantee of 7/0.00 lakh or a

. period of the bank guarantee/property guarantee will be 7 years, and it may need to be extended
* if required.

(%0.00 lakh), ie., 730.00 lakh, a guarantee must be provided on a notarized stamp paper of
