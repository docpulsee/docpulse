06. The Junior Resident shall vacate the accommodation given to him/her as
aforesaid within ten days of the expiry of the term of earlier in the event

Director of the Institute as to whether the Junior Resident has sfactorily

binding on the Junior Resident of the first, second and third year.

09. The working hour of the Junior Resident will not normally exceed

0. The services of the Junior Resident may be terminated as follows:-

(i) By the Director of the Institute without any previous notice if the
Director of the Institute is satisfied on medical evidence that the Junior
Resident is unfit and is likely for a considerable period to continue unfit
by reason of ill-heath for the discharge of his/her duties PROVIDED
ALWAYS the decision of the Director that the Junior Resident is unfit
and is likely to continue unfit shall be conclusive and binding on
him/her.

(ii) By the Director of the Institute without any previous notice if the Junior
Resident is found guilty of any insubordination or intemperance of any
other misconduct or any breach or non-performance of any of the
provisions of the agreement or of any rules pertaining to the Institute. It
is made clear that If Junior Resident strikes work for any reason what so
ever it shall be deemed to be a grave misconduct and his/her
engagement shall be liable to be terminated forth with by the Director of
the Institute PROVIDED ALWAYS that the decision of the Director
of the Institute in the behalf shall be conclusive and binding on him/her.

(iii) By thirty days notice in writing given at any time during service under
this Agreement by the Director to the Institute or its authorized office to
him/her without cause assigned.

PROVIDED ALWAYS that the Director of the Institute may in lieu of any
notice herein provided for, give Junior Resident a sum equivalent to the amount of
his/her salary for thirty days or shorter notice than thirty days if it pay him/her sum
equal to the amount of his/her salary for the period by which such notice falls short
of thirty days. The "Salary" for the purpose of this provision shall mean the Gross
salary Junior Resident would be receiving under clause 3 of this Agreement.
Salary will be release after getting approval of authorities.
