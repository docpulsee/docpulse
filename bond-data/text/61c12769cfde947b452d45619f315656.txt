AND WHEREAS after successful completion of the course of study the bounden shall serve any of
Government Hospital/Health Centre situated in rural/interior areas of the State for a period of two
years anywhere in Arunachal Pradesh within 2(two) years of the completion of the course and also
subject to the terms and conditions hereinafter appearing and the bounden and the sureties have
agreed to the same.


NOW the condition of the above written obligation is that if the Bounden discontinues the study
after taking admission leading to a lapse of seat, the Bounden and sureties shall forthwith pay to the
Govt. of Arunachal Pradesh on demand the total amount of Rs.30, 00,000/- (Rupees Thirty Lakhs only)
or after completion of the MD/MS/DNB/Post Diploma/Post MBBS Diploma course of study to which he/
she was selected, fails to serve the Govt. of Arunachal Pradesh for period of two Years within three
years of completion of course, the Bounden and sureties shall forthwith pay to the Govt. of Arunachal
Pradesh on demand the total amount of Rs.50,00,000/-(Rupees Fifty lakhs only) towards failure to fulfil
the obligation of serving the Government of Arunachal Pradesh in rural/interior areas. The bond is
legally binding on the bounden and the sureties and upon the payment of such sum the above written
obligation shall be void of no effect otherwise this shall remain in full force and effect.

PROVIDED further that the bounden and the sureties do hereby agree that if the Bounden
discontinues the study or after completion of the PG (MD/MS/DNB/Post Diploma/Post MBBS Diploma)
Course of study to which He/She was selected, fails to serve the government of Arunachal Pradesh for
aperiod of two years, it may be construed as professional misconduct and the fact reported to the
National medical Commission or the Arunachal Pradesh Medical Council for suitable action including
cancellation of registration by the Council.

PROVIDED further that the bounden and the sureties do hereby agree that all sums found due to
the Govt. of Arunachal Pradesh under or by virtue of this bond shall be recovered jointly and severally
from them and their properties movable and immovable as if such dues were arrears of land revenue
under the provisions of Revenue Recovery Act for the time being in force or in such other manner as
the Government may deem fit.

Provided further that it is necessary for the Government to sue the bond holder before taking
action on the surety, under this bond and the liabilities of the sureties is co-extensive with that of the
Bounden and shall not be affected by the Government giving time or any other indigence to the
bounden or by the Government varying of the terms and conditions herein contained,

Signed this. Day of. In the Year by the
bounden Shri/Smti.
